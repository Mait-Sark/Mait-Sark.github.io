Welcome. Nice to meet you.

I am a Ph.D. Student working in the domain of Quantum Computing, Quantum Simulation, and Quantum Chemistry.

Previously, I have explored the domain of quantum information processing (bipartite and tripartite states and inequalities), but my recent interest has shifted to the application of quantum computing in quantum chemistry. I am pursuing my Ph.D. in quantum computing and its application in quantum chemistry. The target of my work is to understand the world of chemistry using quantum mechanics embedded in the calculation methods, as qubits are inherently quantum-mechanical. As Feynman has said, "Nature isn't classical, dammit, and if you want to make a simulation of nature, you'd better make it quantum mechanical".

Originally from Jalpaiguri, a small town at the foothills of the Himalayas, I moved to Jodhpur, Rajasthan, to study Chemistry at the Indian Institute of Technology, Jodhpur, and later pursued my Ph.D. in quantum computing and quantum chemistry at the same institute. An avid nature enthusiast, I enjoy walking inside the beautiful campus of IITJ most mornings and exploring the flora and fauna. I still have a passion for travel and hope to visit new places every year. I'm always open to new experiences, cultures, and ways of thinking.
